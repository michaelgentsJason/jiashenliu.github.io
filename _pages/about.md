My research focuses on **embodied and agentic AI**, bringing together geometric perception, visual understanding, and long-horizon reasoning. I work on underwater robotic perception and simulation, video and vision-language models, and LLM agents for reliable tool use and execution.

I received an **MSc with Distinction in Cyber Security Engineering** from the [University of Warwick](https://warwick.ac.uk/) in 2025, and a Bachelor of Management in Information Management and Information Systems from [Nanjing Agricultural University](https://www.njau.edu.cn/) in 2024.
