Hi! I'm **Jiashen Liu** (刘嘉申), a researcher passionate about building intelligent systems that can perceive, reason, and act in the real world.

My research sits at the intersection of **embodied AI**, **computer vision**, and **agentic systems**. I'm particularly interested in:

🤖 **Embodied AI & Robotics**  
Developing perception systems for challenging environments — from underwater caves to complex 3D spaces. I work on visual-inertial navigation, simulation-driven adaptation, and multi-sensor fusion for robust autonomous systems.

🎥 **Video & Vision-Language Understanding**  
Exploring how geometric perception and spatial reasoning can enhance video-language models. How do AI systems truly "see" video beyond frame sequences?

🧠 **LLM Agents & Tool Use**  
Building reliable agentic systems with long-horizon planning, self-evolution capabilities, and intelligent tool orchestration. From automated security testing to multimodal retrieval.
