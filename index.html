Jiashen Liu

Hi, I'm Jiashen, a Research Assistant at PAIRS Lab, The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou). Previously, I was a Research Intern at Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen. I received my MSc in Cyber Security Engineering with Distinction from the University of Warwick, and my bachelor's degree from Nanjing Agricultural University.

My research focuses on embodied intelligence and real-to-sim-to-real systems, particularly reconstructing and adapting realistic simulation environments for embodied agents. I am also interested in 3D computer vision, geometric perception, simulation-driven learning for robotics, and multimodal and LLM-based agents.
