Jiashen Liu

Hi, I'm Jiashen, a Research Assistant at PAIRS Lab, The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou). Previously, I was a Research Intern at Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen. I received my MSc in Cyber Security Engineering with Distinction from the University of Warwick and my bachelor's degree in Information Management and Information Systems from Nanjing Agricultural University. My research focuses on embodied intelligence and real-to-sim-to-real systems, with interests in 3D computer vision, geometric perception, simulation-driven learning for robotics, and multimodal and LLM-based agents.
